How to prepare for a high-quality Zoom training session

A well-positioned camera, enough space and a stable connection make online coaching clearer and more effective.

Online trainingJuly 28, 20265 min read

A little preparation before the call helps the coach see your movement more clearly and allows you to focus on the session.

Position the camera diagonally

A wider frame that captures the whole body is usually more useful than a front view alone. The device should be stable and placed far enough away.

Check the space and lighting

Remove objects that restrict movement. Light should come from the front or side, not directly from behind you.

Prepare your equipment before the session

Your mat, blocks, band, weights or any other agreed equipment should be within reach so that the flow of the session is not interrupted.

Note: This article is educational and does not replace an individual assessment or medical advice.

What to do next
