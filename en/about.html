Introductory consultation

The goal is not simply to complete a workout, but to understand movement.

A professional approach that connects individualisation, sound technique and long-term sustainability.

Training philosophy

Less randomness. More clear reasoning behind every choice.

Every exercise, set and progression should have a purpose in relation to your goal.

The program is built around the person: their experience, abilities, schedule and context. Two people with the same goal therefore do not necessarily need the same plan.

A good program is not the one that looks the hardest. It is the one you can follow consistently and use to make meaningful progress.

Principles of collaboration

  • communication without unnecessary technical jargon
  • realistic expectations without guaranteed outcomes
  • respect for individual limitations and pace
  • clear progression criteria
  • a program that can be carried out in real conditions
