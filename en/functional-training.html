Functional training

Strength is an ability built through high-quality movement.

The goal is not to combine exercises at random, but to systematically develop patterns that support everyday and sporting demands.

The program connects technique, volume, intensity and recovery. Exercises are selected for the person and goal, not for how impressive they look on social media.

What the practice covers

  • squat, hip hinge, push and pull patterns
  • trunk stability and pelvic control
  • mobility required for high-quality execution
  • balance and coordination
  • gradual increases in load
  • exercise variations based on experience and equipment
Important note: A training program is not a substitute for a medical examination, diagnosis, physiotherapy or rehabilitation. In the case of pain, injury or health limitations, consult an appropriate healthcare professional first.

How the collaboration works

The collaboration is structured without being rigid. The program adapts to your feedback, available time and rate of progress.
